BulletJournal Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to BulletJournal. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we look after your personal data when you use our application and tell you about your privacy rights.

2. Data We Collect

BulletJournal is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Journal Entries: Your journal entries, tasks, and notes are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.
  • Photos (Optional): If you choose to add photos to your journal, we request access to your photo library. These images remain on your device.
  • Location (Optional): If you choose to tag entries with location, we request your location data only when you explicitly use this feature.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data solely for the purpose of providing you with the BulletJournal application functionality:

  • Displaying your journal entries in the app
  • Allowing you to organize and manage your tasks
  • Providing location tagging when requested

We do not use your data for marketing purposes, nor do we share it with third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

All your journal data is stored locally on your device. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

5. Your Rights

You have control over your data:

  • You can access, edit, or delete your journal entries at any time within the app
  • You can revoke photo or location permissions through your device settings

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
