Frequently Asked Questions

Does BulletJournal work offline?

Yes! Once you've loaded the app, it works completely offline. Your journal entries are stored locally on your device.

Do I need an Apple Pencil?

While BulletJournal is optimized for Apple Pencil, you can use it with your finger or any iPad-compatible stylus.

How do I export my journal?

Go to Settings > Export and choose your preferred format (PDF, HTML, or plain text).

Can I sync between devices?

Currently, BulletJournal stores data locally on your device. Multi-device sync will be available in a future update.

How do I recover deleted entries?

Check the Trash section within the app. Entries remain there for 30 days before being permanently deleted.
